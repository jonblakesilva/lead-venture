Most service businesses react to the seasons instead of planning for them. The companies that consistently fill their schedule months out are the ones running campaigns *before* demand spikes, not during it.

## Know Your Season Before It Hits

Map out your last two years of revenue by month. You'll see a clear pattern: when jobs pick up, when they slow down, and when customers start researching before they call. Your marketing should lead that curve by 4 to 6 weeks.

For example: if HVAC tune-ups peak in September, your email campaign should go out in mid-August. If spring cleanup is your busiest period, you want to be showing up in search results in late February.

## Run Promotions in the Shoulder Season

The worst time to offer a discount is during peak season (you don't need to). The best time is in your shoulder months, when you want to smooth out revenue and keep your crew busy.

A simple "book now for [Month], limited slots available" offer with a modest discount or free add-on is enough to pull jobs forward and fill your calendar.

## Pre-Season Email Campaigns

If you've been collecting customer emails (and you should be), your slow season is the perfect time to reach out to past customers. A simple message: *"We're booking [service] for [season]. Past customers get first access"* will generate bookings from people who already trust you.

Email is the highest-ROI channel for this because the list is yours. You don't pay per click.

## Seasonal Landing Pages for SEO

Create dedicated pages for your most seasonal services: "spring lawn cleanup in [City]" or "fall HVAC tune-up in [City]." These pages can rank for high-intent searches and pull in new customers who haven't heard of you before.

Keep them live year-round and update them each season. A page that's been live for two years will outrank a new page every time.

## Slow Season = Positioning Season

When calls slow down, double down on:

- **Google review requests** from recent customers
- **Before/after photos** from your best jobs this season
- **Content:** FAQ pages, how-it-works guides, comparison articles
- **Profile updates** on Google Business, Angi, HomeAdvisor, and Yelp

The work you do in your slow season determines how fast you fill up in your busy season.
