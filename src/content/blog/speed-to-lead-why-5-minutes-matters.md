Most contractors think they lose jobs on price. In reality, they lose most of them on speed.

When a homeowner has a leaking pipe, a dead AC unit, or a tree branch through their fence, they aren't calling one company — they're calling three or four and hiring whoever answers first with a real plan. If you're not that company, it doesn't matter how good your work is. You never got the chance to prove it.

## The numbers behind speed to lead

Industry data on inbound lead response is consistent across trades: leads contacted within 5 minutes are dramatically more likely to convert than leads contacted even 30 minutes later. Once you cross the 30-minute mark, conversion rates fall off a cliff. Most homeowners have already booked someone else by then.

Here's the part that should sting: most local service businesses don't respond in 5 minutes. Not because they don't care, but because they're on a ladder, under a sink, or driving between job sites when the call comes in. The lead goes to voicemail, and the homeowner moves to the next name on their list.

## Why missed calls are the leak, not slow marketing

A lot of contractors spend money trying to generate more leads before fixing what happens to the leads they already have. That's backwards. If you're missing even one in five calls and not following up fast, you're paying to generate leads that evaporate before your team ever talks to them.

The fix isn't "answer every call" — that's not realistic when you're running a crew. The fix is making sure a missed call never goes silent:

- **Automatic text-back on missed calls.** The instant you miss a call, the caller gets a text letting them know you saw it and will follow up, with a way to reply right there.
- **Instant lead-form follow-up.** Same principle for web form submissions — an automated first-touch response within seconds, not hours.
- **A simple internal alert.** Whoever owns sales gets notified immediately so a human can pick up the conversation while it's still hot.

## What this actually changes

The businesses that fix speed to lead don't necessarily spend more on ads. They just stop losing the leads they're already paying for. That's usually the fastest, cheapest win available to a local service business — faster than a new website, faster than a rebrand, faster than chasing a new marketing channel.
