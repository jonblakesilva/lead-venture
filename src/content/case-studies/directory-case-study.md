Most local service directories are either outdated, cluttered with irrelevant national chains, or so poorly built that nobody actually finds them through search. We built [HomeProsOhio.com](https://homeprosohio.com) to fix that — a directory built specifically for local, service-based businesses across Ohio and Michigan, designed to actually rank and actually send real eyeballs to the contractors listed on it.

## The problem we're solving

Good local contractors — roofers, landscapers, HVAC techs, tree services, and other home service pros — often have no real online visibility beyond their own website and maybe a Google Business Profile. They're competing against big national lead-gen sites that don't care about the local business at all, they just resell the lead to the highest bidder.

HomeProsOhio is built the opposite way: it's a directory that exists to promote the actual local businesses on it, not to sell their information off to competitors.

## What it does

- **Free and premium listings.** Any legitimate local service business can claim a free listing to get found. Premium providers get additional visibility, placement, and promotional features to stand out further.
- **Built for search visibility.** The directory itself is structured and optimized so it ranks in search, meaning the businesses listed on it get an extra channel of visibility beyond their own website.
- **Growing daily.** We're actively adding new service-based businesses across Ohio and Michigan every day, not letting it sit static like most directories do.

## Why this matters

A directory is only useful if people actually visit it and if the businesses on it actually benefit from being there. HomeProsOhio was built with both of those in mind — it's not a static list, it's an active, growing platform meant to put good local contractors in front of the people searching for them.
