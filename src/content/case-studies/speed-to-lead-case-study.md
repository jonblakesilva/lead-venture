Before working with us, this client was responding to new leads whenever someone got around to it. After putting automated speed-to-lead systems in place, that response time dropped to under 30 seconds.

## Why this matters more than almost anything else

Speed to lead isn't just a nice-to-have metric, it's one of the most heavily studied and consistently proven factors in whether a lead ever becomes a customer.

Research from Harvard Business Review and MIT found that responding to a new lead within 5 minutes makes a business **100 times more likely to make contact** and **21 times more likely to qualify that lead** compared to waiting 30 minutes. And once you're past that window, it only gets worse — companies that respond within one hour are still 7 times more likely to qualify the lead than those that wait 24 hours, and 60 times more likely than businesses that wait a full day.

Most businesses treating leads as something to get back to "when they have time" are fundamentally misaligned with what people actually expect once they've reached out. An 8-hour response time isn't just slow, it's effectively invisible to a customer who already moved on to the next business in their search results.

## What was happening before

- Leads came in through the website, calls, and forms with no automated first response
- Whoever was available (or remembered to check) would follow up, often hours later
- By the time contact was made, many leads had already called a competitor or lost interest entirely

## What we did

1. **Built an instant automated response system.** The moment a lead comes in, whether from a missed call, a form fill, or a chat message, an automated message goes out immediately acknowledging them and starting the conversation.
2. **Routed leads to the right person instantly.** Instead of sitting in a shared inbox waiting for someone to notice, leads are automatically routed and flagged so a real follow-up happens fast, not just an autoresponder and silence.
3. **Layered in missed call text-back.** Any call that isn't answered live triggers an immediate text, so even a missed call turns into an active conversation instead of a dead end.
4. **Set up ongoing monitoring.** Response time isn't a one-time fix, it's tracked and maintained so it doesn't quietly drift back to the old normal.

## The result

| Metric | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average lead response time | 8+ hours | Under 30 seconds |

## Why this matters

**78% of customers buy from the first business that responds to them.** An 8-hour delay doesn't just risk losing a lead, it hands that customer directly to whichever competitor happened to respond first. Going from 8 hours to under 30 seconds doesn't just improve a metric on a dashboard, it puts this business in position to win the vast majority of the leads it's already paying to generate, instead of quietly losing them to someone who simply answered first.
