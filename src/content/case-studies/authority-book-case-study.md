For a home exteriors client, we wrote, designed, and printed a real homeowner's workbook: *Roof Repair or Rip-Off? The Homeowner's Guide to Spotting Scams, Avoiding Costly Mistakes, and Getting It Done Right*, authored under the owner's own name. It's a physical book, not a PDF lead magnet — something a homeowner holds in their hands.

## The problem it solves

Anyone can hand out a business card or a glossy tri-fold at an inspection. A homeowner forgets both by the time the truck pulls away, and neither does anything to separate the owner from every other contractor who's been through that kitchen making the same promises.

## How it works

- **It's handed over at the door.** While the crew does the inspection and takes measurements, the homeowner is reading the owner's own book on how to avoid getting ripped off on exactly the job they're about to get quoted on.
- **It builds instant authority.** A homeowner doesn't ask "can I trust this guy" the same way once he's the published author of the guide sitting in their hands — the credibility is established before the estimate is even written up.
- **It has intermediate CTAs built in.** The book isn't just educational filler — it's built with calls to action woven through it, so a homeowner who reads it cover to cover is guided toward booking, not just informed.
- **It's a built-in PR and media hook.** "Local contractor writes homeowner's guide to avoiding roofing scams" is a far easier pitch to a local newscast, directory, or news site than "local contractor advertises," and every feature or listing that picks it up points back to the book and the business behind it.

## Why this matters

Every home exteriors company competes on the same three things at the door: trust, price, and speed. A published, physical authority book changes the trust equation before a single word of the sales pitch is spoken — and it's a reusable asset that keeps generating PR, referrals, and credibility long after the first print run. It's the same "publish the playbook" strategy behind our own book, [Booked Solid](/about/team), applied as a done-for-you asset for a client's brand instead of ours.
