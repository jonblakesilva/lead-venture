A landscaping business with 12 years in operation came to us looking to grow beyond their existing customer base. Rather than relying on ads or inbound leads, we built a targeted cold outreach system focused entirely on one high-value segment: commercial property managers. Between April and August 2026, that effort generated $513,271 in new, previously non-existent revenue.

## The strategy

Instead of casting a wide net, we went narrow and aggressive on the exact decision-makers who control the biggest, most consistent landscaping contracts — apartment complex and commercial property managers.

- **Door-to-door outreach.** Physically visiting properties and speaking directly with the property manager whenever possible.
- **Cold SMS with a personal touch.** If the property manager wasn't there, we'd take a photo standing in front of their building and text it directly to them — an immediate, unmistakable signal that we'd actually shown up in person.
- **Cold LinkedIn outreach.** For managers who were more responsive digitally, direct messages on LinkedIn opened the same conversation.
- **Persistent, repeated follow-up.** Property managers are busy and hard to pin down, so this wasn't a one-and-done approach.

## The numbers behind the effort

- Visited 250+ apartment buildings, 3 to 6 times each, between January and March
- Sat down for real conversations with 40+ property managers
- Closed 26 commercial properties under contract for the season, including snow removal contracts

That's the kind of volume it actually takes to break into commercial landscaping contracts. It's not a single call or a single drop-in, it's showing up repeatedly until a property manager takes the meeting and takes you seriously.

## The result

| Metric | Result |
| --- | --- |
| New commercial properties under contract | 26 |
| Total new revenue (Apr–Aug 2026) | $513,271 |
| Revenue type | 100% new business |

This wasn't existing customers spending more — it's entirely new revenue from contracts that didn't exist before this outreach started. It covers weekly lawn maintenance, landscaping, hardscaping, and snow contracts, giving this 12-year-old business a substantial, diversified new revenue stream heading into the next season.

## Why this matters

Commercial property contracts are some of the most valuable, recurring revenue a landscaping business can land, but they don't come from waiting on inbound leads. Property managers respond to persistence and to actually showing up. This business had 12 years of credibility and skill already — what was missing was a system to get in front of the right decision-makers consistently enough to close the deal. Once that system was in place, the results followed.
